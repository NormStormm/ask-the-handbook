# Kaimana Demo Resort: Lanai Grill Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs)

*Sample procedures for a fictitious restaurant, written for a software demo.*

## S1 Opening checklist (servers)
Arrive 15 minutes before the doors open at 7 am for breakfast or 11 am for lunch. Check the reservation book and the 86 list (items out of stock) on the kitchen whiteboard. Set tables: napkins, silverware, salt, pepper and sugar caddies. Fill ice wells and brew coffee and iced tea. Turn on the POS terminals and check that the receipt printers have paper. Attend the 10-minute pre-shift meeting.

## S2 Closing checklist (servers)
Close all checks in the POS; no open tabs at the end of a shift. Restock your section: napkins, silverware rolls, condiments. Wipe tables and chairs, and run your side work as assigned on the side-work chart. Turn in tips and credit card slips to the manager for the checkout report. Clock out only after the manager signs your checkout sheet.

## S3 Food safety temperatures
Cold food must be held at 41°F or below; hot food at 135°F or above. Check and log walk-in cooler and freezer temperatures at opening and closing on the clipboard by the door. If the walk-in reads above 41°F, tell the chef or MOD immediately and do not serve food from it until it is checked. Reheat leftovers to 165°F within 2 hours.

## S4 Handwashing
Wash hands for at least 20 seconds with soap and warm water: before starting work, after using the restroom, after touching your face or phone, after handling raw meat or seafood, after taking out trash, and when changing gloves. Use the handwashing sink only; never the food prep or dish sinks.

## S5 Voids and comps in the POS
Servers cannot void an item after it is sent to the kitchen. Ask a manager to void it and give the reason (kitchen error, guest changed mind, etc.). Comps up to $15 under the LEARN policy (handbook section 5.1) need a manager's approval code in the POS. All voids and comps appear on the nightly manager report.

## S6 Handling cash
Count your bank at the start and end of the shift with a manager present. Never leave cash unattended. Report any shortage over $5 to the manager before clocking out.

## S7 Opening the bar
Check the par sheet and restock beer, wine and spirits from the liquor room (manager key only). Cut fruit garnishes fresh each day and label them with the date. Test the ice machine and the soda gun. Check that the bar's ID guide and the 21+ signage are posted.
